SMS & Messaging Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Overview

CRRE Labs LLC ("we," "us," "our") may send marketing SMS text messages to individuals and businesses that have separately opted in to receive marketing messages from us. This policy describes what messages you may receive, how you can opt in and opt out, and your rights regarding our messaging practices.

2. Consent & Opt-In

SMS opt-in is collected through the contact form on crrelabs.com only. The SMS consent checkbox is for marketing messages only and is separate from submitting a contact or inquiry form. Checking the box is optional, unchecked by default, and is the only way to opt in to SMS. Providing a phone number or submitting an inquiry does not constitute SMS consent. Inquiry follow-up is by email.

By checking the separate marketing SMS checkbox and providing your mobile number, you expressly consent to receive marketing SMS from CRRE Labs LLC about web design, AI automation, demos, and promotional offers. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

The only active opt-in method is:

  • Checking the separate marketing SMS checkbox on the contact form at crrelabs.com and submitting a valid mobile number

Keyword opt-in (for example texting START or INFO), verbal requests, paper forms, and other non-website methods are not active and are not used for this messaging program. Do not treat those methods as a way to join.

3. Types of Messages

Messages from this program are marketing and promotional only. They may include:

  • Promotional offers: Information about web design, AI automation, and related services
  • Demos and showcases: Demo invitations and portfolio showcases
  • Marketing updates: New features and special offers

This program does not send transactional, account, rental, payment, or inquiry-reply SMS.

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 5 marketing messages per month. We will not send excessive or unsolicited messages.

5. Opt-Out

You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message from us
  • Emailing carlos@crrelabs.com with the subject "Unsubscribe SMS"
  • Calling us at (786) 733-6744

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and will no longer receive marketing SMS from this program.

6. Help

For assistance with our messaging program, reply HELP to any message, email carlos@crrelabs.com, or call (786) 733-6744.

7. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. CRRE Labs does not charge for text messages, but your mobile carrier may charge standard messaging fees. Check with your carrier for details about your messaging plan.

8. Supported Carriers

Our messaging services are compatible with most major U.S. carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

9. Privacy

Your phone number and messaging data are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. Your information is used solely to provide the services described in this policy.
