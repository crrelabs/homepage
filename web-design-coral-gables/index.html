Coral Gables website design, without pretending we are on the Mile

Coral Gables businesses on Miracle Mile and the downtown grid sit next to neighbors that already look considered: restaurants, salons, galleries, medical and dental offices, and professional firms. A customer on the Mile compares you with the door they just passed. The site does not have to be loud. It does have to be specific: what you do, when you are open, and how to reach the front desk.

CRRE Labs is not a Gables storefront. The studio is in Pinecrest, at 12394 SW 82nd Ave, south of the Gables. Owner Carlos Rovira. Phone (786) 733-6744. If you want a firm with a Miracle Mile lobby, say so and this is probably the wrong fit. If you want the published packages and a local phone, this is the page.

Which package, without the upsell script

Starter, $99 setup and $15 a month, is one page. It is a reasonable fit for a practice or a shop that needs hours, a service list, and a form, and does not need a separate page per procedure.

Business, $199 and $29 a month, goes to five pages and adds click-to-call, basic on-page SEO, and Google Business Profile optimization, plus one revision a month. That is the package most Gables offices are comparing when they want the profile and the site to tell the same story.

Pro, $349 and $49 a month, adds a booking widget or a Stripe embed, an analytics report, and one or two content updates a month. Use it if the front desk already has a booking tool that can be embedded. We do not pick a medical-records system for you.

A custom site closer to the apartment projects in the portfolio is a separate quote. Those were not $99 jobs. See the Miami apartment case study.

What we will not imply

We do not claim a specialty in law firm marketing, medical compliance, or luxury branding. A marketing page can list your services in your words. It does not make the practice compliant, and it does not review your ads for professional rules.

Local competitors mostly start at $300–$500. Our package prices are lower and the scope is written down. A Gables quote above that range may be for photography, a brand system, or more pages than Pro includes. Compare the list, not just the first number.

Questions

Will the site look like every other small-business template?

The packages are straightforward sites: your facts, your photos, the phone, and the pages the package allows. They are not a custom brand campaign. If you need that, ask for a custom quote and look at the portfolio first.

Can you meet in the Gables?

We can take the project by phone or video from the Pinecrest studio. Call (786) 733-6744. We do not list a Gables meeting office.

Do you build sites for Miracle Mile restaurants?

Yes, as restaurant sites: hours, menu, address, phone. Read the restaurants page for what is included and what is not. Reservations, if you want a booking embed, are a Pro feature, described there as a booking widget, not as a named reservation network.

Is the Google profile required?

No. If you have one, Business and Pro include optimization so the profile and the site agree. The category on our own profile is Website designer, which is separate from your category.
