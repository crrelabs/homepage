What Iris Handles

Every repetitive task your PM team dreads — automated, tracked, and escalated without human intervention.

Automated Rent Collections

3-tier daily escalation system. Iris sends reminders, follows up on late payments, and escalates to notices — all automatically, every single day.

Delinquency Tracking

Real-time tracking of who owes what, how long it's been outstanding, and what action has been taken. No tenant slips through the cracks.

Property Onboarding

New properties get set up in Buildium automatically — units, tenants, lease terms, and payment schedules configured without manual data entry.

Bill Entry & Processing

Bills from Monday.com are processed and entered into your accounting system. No copy-pasting, no missed invoices, no human error.

Document Generation

Leases, addendums, notices, and renewals generated on demand. Iris pulls the data, fills the templates, and delivers ready-to-sign documents.

Email Automation

Tenant communications, owner reports, vendor coordination — Iris reads, drafts, and sends emails based on triggers and schedules you define.

System Integrations

Google Drive, Monday.com, Buildium, email — Iris connects your existing tools so data flows between them without manual transfers.

Section 8 Inspections

Tracks inspection schedules, deadlines, and compliance requirements for Section 8 properties. Never miss a housing authority deadline again.

Deal Due Diligence

Evaluating a new acquisition? Iris pulls comps, runs numbers, and organizes due diligence documents so you can make faster, smarter decisions.
