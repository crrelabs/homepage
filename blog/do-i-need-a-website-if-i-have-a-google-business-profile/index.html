Do I need a website if I already have a Google Business Profile?

Often, yes. A Google Business Profile is the Maps listing: hours, phone, address, photos, and reviews. A website is the page you edit, with room for a menu, a service list, or a form. One does not replace the other.

What the listing does well

People who already know the neighborhood can find the pin, see the hours, and tap the phone. Reviews live there too. Ours are 5.0 from 24 Google reviews. A listing can be enough for a while if all you need is the phone to ring.

Where a website helps

  • A full menu, a list of services, or several trades that will not fit in a short description.
  • The plaza, the suite, and the cities you drive to, spelled out.
  • A form for people who will write and will not call.
  • Prices you are ready to publish. Ours are on the site because there is room for three plans and what each one includes.
  • A page on your own domain, for as long as you keep it.

Use them together

Point the listing at the site. Put the same name, address, and phone on both. When the hours disagree, the customer does not know which Thursday to trust. Business ($199 setup and $29 a month) and Pro ($349 setup and $49 a month) include that alignment. Starter ($99 setup and $15 a month) is the site itself.

That work means the facts match and the pages are written in ordinary language. It is not a promise about where you rank.

Reviews are not the whole story

Reviews help people choose you. They do not lay out private events, a second location, or a separate path for an owner and an applicant. Property managers feel that quickly. The property-manager page shows the difference between a small office site and a custom building site.

Questions

Does a Google listing replace a website?

They do different jobs. The listing helps people find you on Maps. The website is where you explain the services, the menu, or the next step in as much detail as you need.
