Do I need a website if I already have a Google Business Profile?

Often, yes, but not because a profile is useless. A Google Business Profile is the listing Google shows on Maps: category, hours, phone, address, photos, and reviews. A website is the page you edit, with room for a menu, a service list, a second phone number, or a form. One does not contain the other.

What the profile is good at

People who already know the neighborhood can find the pin, see the hours, and tap the phone. Reviews sit on the profile. Ours, for example, is a Website designer profile at 5.0 from 24 Google reviews. That fact lives on the profile. It is not something a website package creates, and this article does not tell you how to get the same rating.

If your whole public story is a phone number, a category, and hours, the profile may be enough for now. A food truck with a changing corner, or a crew that only wants the phone to ring, can stay there until the story gets longer. That is a real option. It is not a consolation prize.

What the profile is awkward at

  • A full menu, a list of procedures, or a set of trades that do not fit in a short description.
  • Explaining which plaza, which suite, and which cities you drive to, in more than a line.
  • A contact form that is not the same click as the phone. Some customers will write and will not call.
  • Publishing prices. Our website prices are public because the site has room for them. A profile is a poor place for three packages and the exclusions.
  • Owning the page. The profile is a Google product. The site is yours, on your domain, for as long as you keep the domain and the host.

Use them together

The profile should link to the site. The site should show the same name, address, and phone as the profile. When those disagree, the customer does not know which Thursday hours to trust. On our Business package ($199 setup and $29 a month) and Pro package ($349 setup and $49 a month), basic on-page SEO and Google Business Profile optimization are included so that check is part of the work. Starter ($99 setup and $15 a month) is the site only: one page, a form, domain, SSL, and hosting. It does not include the profile line.

Optimization here means aligning the facts and writing the pages in ordinary language. It does not mean a promise about where you rank. We do not sell that.

A website is not automatic once you have reviews

Reviews help the profile. They do not list your services in the order you want, and they do not give you a page for private events, a second location, or an owner inquiry that should not go to the leasing line. Property managers hit this quickly: the profile is the office, and the site is where an owner and an applicant can be sent to different explanations. See the property-manager page for how that differs from a custom building site.
