How much does a website cost in Miami in 2026?

A small-business website from CRRE Labs is $99, $199, or $349 to set up, then $15, $29, or $49 a month. Many other local quotes start around $300 to $500. Here is what our prices buy, and what to ask before you compare a single number.

Three ways to start

The studio is at 12394 SW 82nd Ave, Pinecrest, FL 33156. Call (786) 733-6744. The price is the same in Pinecrest, Kendall, South Miami, Coral Gables, Palmetto Bay, and Miami.

  • Starter — $99 setup + $15/mo. One page, a contact form, and hosting with the domain and SSL. If you leave and come back within 90 days, reactivation is $49.
  • Business — $199 setup + $29/mo. Up to five pages, click-to-call, basic on-page SEO, help with your Google listing, and one revision a month. Reactivation within 90 days is $99.
  • Pro — $349 setup + $49/mo. Up to ten pages, plus a booking or payment embed, an analytics report, and one or two content updates a month. Reactivation within 90 days is $175.

Every plan is month to month. Setup is paid up front and stays with us once design begins. You can pay the setup on the pricing page.

What a higher quote might include

A $450 quote is not automatically a worse deal. It may cover more pages, photography, or a custom build. A lower quote may be a page you rent. Ask who owns the domain, whether hosting is included, how edits work after launch, and what happens if you cancel. Ours is month to month, with the reactivation fees above if you return within 90 days.

Our $99 plan is real, and it is focused: one page, hosting included, no monthly revision. If you want five pages and a Google listing that matches the site, the number to compare is $199 to set up and $29 a month.

When the price changes

  • Page count: one, up to five, or up to ten.
  • On-page SEO and Google listing help, which start on Business.
  • Booking, payments, an analytics report, or monthly edits, which are Pro.
  • Work outside the card. Leasing sites, assistants, and 3D printing are quoted on their own. The stories show the kind of custom web work we take on.

Call (786) 733-6744, use the form on the homepage, or check out a package. If you run a restaurant, a crew, a salon, an office, or a property business, the industry pages explain which plan tends to fit.
