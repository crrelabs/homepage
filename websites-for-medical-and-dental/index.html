Medical and dental marketing sites, clearly not a patient portal

A dental or medical office site, for this studio, is a marketing page. It shows the address that matches your Google Business Profile, the phone, the hours, the kind of practice, and what a new patient should expect if you give us that text. It is not a patient portal, not an insurance checker, and not a HIPAA compliance program.

Kendall has a thick cluster of offices around the hospital corridor. Coral Gables has practices on and off Miracle Mile. Pinecrest and Palmetto Bay have smaller offices that live on referrals. The website work is the same three packages. The compliance work is not included, because we do not sell it.

What to put on the page

Name, suite, hours, and phone, identical to the Google profile. Business and Pro include Google Business Profile optimization, which is the pass that checks those facts against the site.

The services you want a new patient to understand, in plain language you approve. We do not write clinical claims, outcome promises, or insurance acceptance unless you supply the sentence and own it.

Parking or suite instructions if the building is confusing. That is ordinary content, and it prevents the phone call that is only 'which tower?'

A form for general questions. Do not use it for records, images, or symptoms you would put in a chart. Say that on the page. If someone needs care, they call the office.

What we will not say we do

We will not say the site makes the practice HIPAA compliant. A brochure website is not a HIPAA program. Business associate agreements, chart systems, and email of records are outside these packages.

We will not claim a connection to a specific electronic health record. Pro can embed a booking widget if your front desk uses one that embeds. If it does not embed, Pro does not magically integrate it.

Local competitors mostly start at $300–$500. Our Starter setup is $99. A quote that includes photography, copy from a medical writer, or a portal will not match a $99 one-page site. Read the card.

Questions

Will this make our office HIPAA compliant?

No. The site is marketing. Do not send medical records, images, or detailed symptoms through the contact form. Call the office for anything clinical.

Can new patients book online?

Pro includes a booking widget or Stripe embed if you already have a tool that can be embedded. It does not include an electronic health record.

Can you list the insurance we take?

Only the list you give us, and you are responsible for keeping it true. We do not verify benefits.

Which package do offices usually need?

There is no usual. A single page with hours and phone is Starter, $99 + $15/mo. Separate pages for the services you want found, plus profile alignment, is Business, $199 + $29/mo, up to five pages. Booking and monthly content updates are Pro, $349 + $49/mo.
