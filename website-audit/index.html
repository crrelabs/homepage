What We Audit

Eight areas that decide whether people find you, trust you, and actually get in touch.

Speed

We measure how fast your key pages load on desktop and mobile and find what slows them down: oversized images, render-blocking scripts, heavy plugins, missing caching, and Core Web Vitals issues.

Mobile

We check every key page on phone and tablet sizes for layouts that break, text that’s too small, buttons that are hard to tap, and menus that don’t work.

SSL & security

We confirm your SSL certificate is valid, every page redirects to HTTPS, there’s no mixed content, and we flag outdated software, exposed admin pages, and missing security headers.

SEO

We review page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, indexing settings, your sitemap and robots.txt, and whether Google Search Console is set up.

Local SEO

We compare your Google Business Profile with your site, check that your business name, address and phone number match everywhere, and look for LocalBusiness schema.

Broken links

We crawl your site for dead internal and outbound links, missing pages, and broken images or downloads.

CTAs & forms

We check that every page has a clear next step, that click-to-call works, and we send test submissions to confirm your contact forms actually reach your inbox.

Design

We look for an outdated look, cluttered layouts, inconsistent branding, and anything that makes a first-time visitor trust you less.

The Optimization Pass

After the audit, we fix the issues it finds, within the scope of the package. Depending on what we find, that can include:

  • Compress and resize images, and lazy-load ones below the fold
  • Remove or defer unused scripts and plugins where your platform allows
  • Fix mobile layout, tap-target, and text-size problems on key pages
  • Fix HTTPS redirects and mixed content, and update what can be safely updated
  • Write or rewrite page titles and meta descriptions, and fix heading structure
  • Add or correct LocalBusiness schema and make your name, address and phone consistent on the site
  • Submit or fix your sitemap and connect Google Search Console
  • Repair or redirect broken links
  • Fix contact forms and click-to-call so inquiries reach you
  • Design touch-ups to key pages: spacing, typography, buttons, and calls to action

The package covers fixing what the audit finds on your existing site. It doesn’t include a full redesign, new pages, writing new page copy, or ongoing maintenance. If a fix is bigger than the package, we’ll list it in the report so you can decide what to do next.

Questions

What do you need from me?

Your website address to start the audit. For the optimization pass we also need login access to your website platform or hosting (for example WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or your host’s control panel), plus access to your Google Business Profile and Google Search Console if you want us to work on those.

What platforms do you work with?

Most common website platforms, including WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, and hand-coded or static sites. Some platforms limit what can be changed, such as speed settings or code. If that’s the case, the report says so and we fix what the platform allows.

What if my site needs a full rebuild?

Sometimes the audit shows a site isn’t worth patching. If so, we’ll tell you, and our Starter, Business, and Pro website packages on the pricing page are the better fit. You can also look at those first if you already know you want a new site.

Is it a one-time payment?

Yes. The Website Audit & Optimization package is $300, paid once. There is no subscription and no monthly fee.

Will you rewrite my content?

We rewrite page titles and meta descriptions, and we may adjust headings and calls to action where it helps. We don’t rewrite your page copy as part of this package. If your copy needs work, the report will point that out.

Do you guarantee rankings or more leads?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee search rankings. The audit finds real problems, and the optimization pass fixes them so your site is faster, works on phones, and gets inquiries to you.
