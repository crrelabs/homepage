What We Audit

Eight areas that decide whether visitors find you, trust you, and actually get in touch.

Speed & performance

Page load times, image sizes, render-blocking scripts, caching, and Core Web Vitals checks on key pages.

Mobile responsiveness

How every key page looks and works on phones and tablets: layout, tap targets, readable text, and menus.

SSL/HTTPS & security basics

Certificate status, HTTPS redirects, mixed content, outdated plugins or scripts, and basic security headers.

SEO basics

Page titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, indexing, sitemap, and Google Search Console setup.

Local SEO basics

Google Business Profile consistency, matching business name, address and phone everywhere, and LocalBusiness schema.

Broken links

Dead internal and outbound links, missing pages, and broken images or downloads.

CTAs & contact forms

Clear calls to action, click-to-call, and test submissions to confirm your forms actually deliver to your inbox.

Design refresh

An outdated look, cluttered layouts, inconsistent branding, and anything that makes visitors trust you less.
