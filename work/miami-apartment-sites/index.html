Downtown 1st

What we can say about the previous site

The CRRE Labs homepage describes this as a full redesign for luxury apartments in Miami's Arts and Entertainment District. The repo does not include a copy of the previous site, so this page does not guess what was wrong with it.

What was built

The public description says the redesign uses the property's photography, a gallery lightbox, and a mobile-first layout. The portfolio page adds a 56-story leasing site with bay and skyline views, amenities, floor plans, and tour scheduling. The live site at downtown1st.com shows those sections: a hero for the rental tower, amenities, a gallery, floor plans, the address 30 SW 1st Street, Miami, FL 33130, and a contact path.
