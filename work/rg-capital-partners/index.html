RG Capital Partners website

A website for RG Capital Partners, a private-equity real estate firm that works in Kansas City and Miami. The CRRE Labs homepage calls it a full redesign built with Tailwind and says it was delivered in under a week. That timing is already published here. This page does not add a second timeline, a fee, or a result.

Open the live site See the screenshot in the portfolio

What we can say about the previous site

The public pages call it a redesign. They do not include the old site. An internal project note in this repo mentions WordPress. That note is not treated as a public claim here, because the portfolio text itself does not say it. The honest public line is: it was a redesign.

What was built

The portfolio describes a dark site with gold accents for a syndication firm, with a property portfolio and an investor contact path. The screenshot shows the top of the homepage: the firm name, links for Home, About, Portfolio, Team, and Contact, an Investor Portal button, the headline Private Equity Real Estate, a Kansas City and Miami line, and buttons labeled View Portfolio and Get in Touch.

The live site publishes its own property statistics. This case study does not repeat those figures. They are the firm's numbers, sources on the page have not been stable enough to copy, and they are not a CRRE Labs result.
